Manual CW transmit

      

        
        
        

          Sends through the ESP32's built-in CW engine at the configured CW speed.
          Additional text is queued; the key automatically releases when the queue drains.
          Maximum 120 characters per request. UART2 must be set to Winkey CW in Configuration.
        

      

      

        
        
      

      

        Abort clears pending CW text and ends transmission through the normal PTT/PA safety sequence.
